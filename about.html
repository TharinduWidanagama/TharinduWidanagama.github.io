About

Hey, I'm Tharindu.

Hey, I guess I'm really bad at doing "myself" speech, because every time, I always think where I should be starting from.

Anyhow, my name is Tharindu Widanagama and I'm currently an undergraduate studying Business Management at Anglia Ruskin University. I actually first started my higher studies with the hope of pursuing a career in Network Engineering field which is why I started following a diploma in Network Engineering, but then, I found that Software Engineering, Network Engineering, Open Source projects, GNU/Linux, etc. are basically my hobbies (specially working on UNIX systems!) and not something I'd be willing to do as a job. I was wandering around and then realized I'd be more happy to teach and mentor others which is why I decided to pursue a career path in Learning & Development, Training and Human Resources. Although, it took me years to finally decide what I want to do, I believe I found my happiness in that industry, while of course tinkering with my computer systems.

Also, having a technical background really helped me to close the gaps when working with employees in every industry because most of the times, employees from one industry find it difficult to make connections with employees in different other industries.


Also, I must mention about the exposure that I got from AIESEC though that deserve a separate blog post (Maybe, by the time you read this, I might have already written and published one in this website). Having to do almost everything in a professional organization was actually a blessing even though many of us were frustrated with the workload. But, the people that we got to meet, and the memories that we had there were nothing but the very best! The creative stuff that I still bring to the trainings and to the workplaces are still heavily influenced by AIESEC. We'll talk about those things later. Almost forgot: I'm a Rotaract Alumnus and also, I was a part of Sasnaka Sansada Foundation as well. I still go to their events whenever I have some free time and it's really amazing to see how far they have come within few years!


Speaking about my academics, apart from the college degrees that I'm following, I try to complete important certifications whenever I have some free time on my hands. Mostly, they are related to the tech industry, but now I'm in the process of gathering qualifications related to operations, trainings and people management. Unfortunately, majority of the working professionals are under the impression that you need a college degree and few paper certificates to properly do any job nowadays which is absolutely wrong in my opinion and I have seen many organizations now leaving those traditional corporate practices and recruit people with experience and atitude over paper qualifications and get success which is actually very happy to see. You may find the full list of my certifications on my LinkedIn.


Also, I have been in a music band called RAW9, but I stepped away from my role as a vocalist and the keyboard player at the end of 2025 because I was hectic and couldn't find time to do practices properly and I didn't want to hold the other members in the band till I'm available. We started the band with 9 talented individuals (which is why we decided to call it as RAW9) and I loved that experience. I was not a kid who stayed inside the classroom during my school days and always in a middle of a practice at the music practicing room. One of my ambitions was and is to pursue a role in the music industry in the future and maybe one day, I will.
