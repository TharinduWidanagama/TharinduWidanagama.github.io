Testing One, Two, Three...

5 min read · Published 16 September 2026

The website is still barebones, but I wanted to publish it as soon as possible because it was a "now or never" situation. If you are reading this, thank you! Probably, there are tons of things that need the attention in this website, but I guess this should do enough for now as the very first start.


There's are MANY reasons why I decided to start a website. Believe me when I say this, but MANY. One of them would be to showcase my talent to the external parties obviously. But that was just the tip of the iceberg. Let's just say that I was heavily inspired to make my own website years ago after coming across this video: "Get a Website Now! Don't be a Web Peasant!" . I don't still think that I'm an "Internet Landlord" because I don't have my own VPS or a domain, but this should be sufficient for now (Probably, in the future! So, if you read this in the future and there's a separate domain, that means target achieved!).

Additionally, I needed to my put technical knowledge in practice because right now, it's so rusty because I have been engaging in other non-technical stuff over the years and you can imagine the rest. The other reason is that I needed to contribute my fair share to go against the enshitification and inspire others to have their own space while helping to keep the internet as it was intended originally.

Also, I needed to declare war against web bloat and soydevs who are basically using JavaScript and all these other bloated frameworks to design websites nowadays which is why I didn't use a single line of JS or any other framework while designing this website and obviously, it's tracking-free and doesn't drain your battery while reading or easy to maintain.

Perfect examples would be : This is a motherfucking website, This is still a motherfucking website, This is the best motherfucking website, This is the final motherfucking website, The best motherfucking website.

Hey, don't look at me, I didn't come up with the names for those websites.

Additional read for those who have no idea what JavaScript means and how to get rid of it: disable-javascript.org/

The other reason is a more Greener Earth. By designing a minimal, suckless website (add the suckless philosophy link here), I wanted to make an example of a low carbon, more sustainable page.

So, those actually are few of major reasons that this website started. Again, thank you very much for visiting this and giving it a look and you may share it with your colleagues and friends as well!

PS: If you want your own website, and you find this website appealing, go ahead and copy it from my Github. no worries, It's open source!
