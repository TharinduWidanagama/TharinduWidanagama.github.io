About

FAQ

Questions I get asked frequently

Why don't you use "Social Media"

I do, but not in the usual way. Actually, I don't to be honest except my LinkedIn page and I have many reasons actually.

Can you run a single workshop, or does it have to be an ongoing engagement?

Either works. A lot of engagements start as a single workshop or audit, and some turn into an ongoing retainer once the first session shows results.

Do you deliver security training even though you're not a security engineer?

Yes, the training I run focuses on behaviour change and habit-building for non-technical staff, which is a training problem more than an engineering one. For deep technical security work, I'll point you to the right specialist.

You are a Trainer, a HR person, how do you know technical stuff?!

Well, I don't actually know lots of stuff. I just know the very basic things any person can learn if they put their efforts. It's a long story, you can actually check my blog posts later about how I came across things and unfortunately, it might take some time, because I'm really having some tough time to write my blog posts :)
